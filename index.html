Our Story

Scandinavian Craft in Every Cup

Founded in Malmö in 2022, Nordic Roast Coffee celebrates the Scandinavian tradition of fika combined with precision specialty roasting. We partner directly with sustainable farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala.
